A newspaper front page summing up the world news of Wednesday 18 November 2026 in one 1024×1024 image, built for make.fun's [news-image-20261118](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261118) bounty.

Lead story: Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin meet in Shenzhen ahead of the historic first Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral summit at APEC. Also covered: the US–Iran escalation in the Gulf, Ukraine disabling the Kerch Bridge, Democrats taking the US Senate, COP31's decisive final days, and severe Cyclone Akvan in India.

Run it: `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py` — writes `out/entry.png`. The image is drawn by `make_image.py` (Pillow) and copied byte-for-byte by `main.py`.
